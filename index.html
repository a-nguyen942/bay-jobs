    Independent watchdog

    This sends a heartbeat to Healthchecks.io only after every source has a successful check in the current hour. That outside service can alert you even when this app stops running.

    1. Create a check on Healthchecks.io using its free plan. Set the period to 1 hour and grace time to 30 minutes.
    2. Enable and verify an email alert destination there.
    3. Paste its hc-ping.com ping URL below. The first heartbeat is sent after a complete hourly scan at the end of the recovery window. Confirm that your check turns green there.

    Open this panel to load watchdog status.

    A connected URL does not confirm that external email alerts are enabled. Configure those in Healthchecks.io. Pause the external check there before disconnecting to avoid a missing-heartbeat alert.
